Purpose and method
Transfection method and reagent are set per plate in the Plate Designer, not globally — different plates in one experiment may legitimately use different chemistry. The brief requires that methods keep independent calculation settings, so no single value is forced here.
Global calculation settings
One excess rule, applied identically to every component. The source workbook ran the ASO tube at +9.4% (wells hand-typed as 35 against a real 32) and the lipid tube at +15.2% ((sum × 1.05) + 1), while appearing to promise 5%. That is now a single number you control, with dead volume named separately instead of hidden as a +1 inside a formula.
Minimum pipetting volume binds hard on dose-response work. From a 400 µM stock into 100 µL wells the oligo volume per well is C × 0.25 µL, so one well falls below 0.4 µL for any target under 1.6 µM. A 35-well master mix buys room down to about 46 nM. Below that an intermediate dilution is mandatory, and the calculator proposes one.

Master Mixes

Derived from the plate layout, never typed. Wells are grouped only when every variable matches — oligo, chemistry, stock, target concentration, reagent and amount, culture volume and protocol. Conditions that differ in any of those are never pooled.

Vessel Formats

Starting values from the workbook's Numbers sheet. Manufacturers differ, so everything here is editable and changes flow straight into the calculators.

Geometry and default volumes
35 mm is listed at 8.8 cm² but 6-well at 9.6 cm². In most catalogues these are the same vessel. Both values are as found in the workbook; one is probably wrong, and the difference moves seeding by about 9%.
Only the 96-well row is measured. It reproduces the workbook exactly (90 µL media + 10 µL mix = 100 µL, 0.3 µL RNAiMAX). Every other row holds conventional working volumes with the mix at 10% of total and reagent at 0.30% v/v, scaled from that one measured row. They are starting points, not validated lab values. Thermo quotes 9 µL RNAiMAX for a 6-well, against the 6.0 derived here.
Dropped from the workbook: DNA amount (312.5 ng/cm²) and Vol Tfec rate (=12.5/C9). An ASO protocol has no ng amount, and the transfection rate is provably unusable — at 96-well it gives a 1.1 µL total mix, 0.55 µL per tube, when RNAiMAX alone is 0.3 µL. Both are plasmid-sheet leftovers.
