How this is stored
Each entry is a Markdown file in experiments/<ID>/notebook/, with pasted images written beside it in notebook/attachments/ and named after a hash of their own bytes. Open the folder in ten years with no browser and the record is still readable. Editing or deleting an entry keeps the superseded text under notebook/.history/ — not an audit trail, just a safety net, so a wrong edit is recoverable. Everything is checksummed in the experiment's manifest.
This is not a compliant ELN. It has no counter-signing and no tamper-evidence against you, because you asked for a working bench record rather than Benchling's ceremony. Anything that needs a signed, audited record still belongs in Labfolder — export the experiment and attach it there.
